# Narrative Issue Corpus

## Is something missing, or is uncertainty doing useful work?

A scene can be quiet without being inert. A character can contradict themselves without being badly written. A missing causal step can also be a real problem, however polished the sentences around it.

The Narrative Issue Corpus is Sheila Studios' research collection for making those distinctions inspectable. It organizes candidate readings of fiction, scripts and story-world material around evidence, context, alternative explanations and possible revision choices. Its intended users are writers, editors and people designing AI-assisted critique.

This is a **curated, documentation-only public edition**, not an autonomous literary judge, validated scoring system or complete corpus release.

### Start with the examples

[Three short worked examples](WORKED-EXAMPLES.md) show different outcomes:

- A physical continuity problem supported by the supplied scene contract.
- An apparently static scene whose uncertainty and restraint are doing work.
- A possible character turn that cannot be judged from an incomplete revision.

All three passages and their annotations were newly written for this edition. They are editorial illustrations, not application output, unpublished manuscript extracts or evidence of measured critique accuracy.

The [issue guide](ISSUE-GUIDE.md) offers a small selection of supported categories and the conditions under which they may be misleading. The [evidence note](EVIDENCE.md) identifies the source materials and open limitations.

### A candidate reading, not a command

A useful critique should point to the passage that prompted it, explain the likely reader effect, and say what would change the assessment. It should distinguish an observed symptom from a proposed cause and preserve an author's option to keep an effect deliberately.

That does not make every judgment equally uncertain. An established object cannot be in two places at once in a realistic continuous scene. But an unanswered emotional question is not the same kind of claim. Severity, confidence, author intent and taste need separate treatment.

### Maturity and relationships

The inspected sources contain category definitions, candidate-record designs, issue descriptions, boundary examples, response templates and a risk review. They do not establish a unified accepted executable schema or generally validated literary judgment. Naming a file a boundary test does not mean a test was run.

This project is distinct from TellTale's agent/session diagnostics and Infinite Manuscript's authoring application. No integration into either product is claimed here.
